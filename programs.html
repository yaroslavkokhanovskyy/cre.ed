Questions

I have no commercial experience. Can I start?

Yes. Open the free library first. Offer 1 is built for agents and career changers, Offer 2 for investors and business owners.

How much time does it take each week?

Plan on about three to five hours: one video, one worksheet and one small action in your market.

Which program should I pick?

Offer 1 teaches you to work as a commercial realtor. Offer 2 teaches you to analyze, finance and buy a building yourself.

What if it is not for me?

Both programs have a 30-day money-back guarantee. Write to us and we refund you.

Is this legal or financial advice?

No. CRE-Ed is educational. Talk to a lawyer, accountant or licensed advisor before you act on a deal.
